About TaskEarn ZM

TaskEarn ZM is a free online platform created in 2026 to help Zambians earn extra income using their mobile phones.

Our Mission

We connect Zambian users with advertisers who need simple tasks completed. These include answering surveys, testing apps, and watching promotional videos. We share the advertising revenue with our users through MTN and Airtel Mobile Money payments.

Why We Started

Many Zambians have smartphones and data bundles but few opportunities to earn online. TaskEarn ZM solves this by offering small, frequent payouts for tasks that take 1-5 minutes. We focus on transparency: no signup fees, no hidden charges, and a low K5.00 minimum cashout.

Beta Status

We are currently in beta testing with users in Kitwe, Ndola, and Lusaka. During beta, we are adding new tasks daily and optimizing our payment system. User feedback helps us improve.
