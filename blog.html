How to Cash Out TaskEarn to MTN & Airtel Money in Zambia

Last updated: April 18, 2026

One of the biggest questions we get from new TaskEarn users in Zambia is: “How do I actually get my money?” The answer is simple. TaskEarn pays directly to MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money. There are no gift cards, no PayPal, and no bank requirements. If you have a phone number, you can get paid.

Step 1: Reach the Minimum Withdrawal of K50

You need a balance of at least K50 in your TaskEarn account to request a payout. Most users hit this after completing 4 to 6 surveys. Surveys pay between K8 and K25 depending on length. Your balance updates instantly after each approved survey, so you always know how close you are to cashing out.

Step 2: Request Your Withdrawal

Once you have K50 or more, go to your TaskEarn dashboard and tap the “Withdraw” button. You will be asked to choose your payment method. Select either MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money. Make sure the phone number you enter is registered for mobile money and is in your name. Typing the wrong number is the #1 reason for delayed payments.

Step 3: Wait for Processing

All withdrawals are reviewed by our team to prevent fraud. This takes 1 to 3 business days. We process payments Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm CAT. Requests made on Saturday or Sunday are sent on Monday. You will receive an SMS confirmation from MTN or Airtel once the money hits your account.

Step 4: Confirm You Got Paid

Check your mobile money balance. The payment will come from “TaskEarn ZM” or one of our payment partners. There are zero fees. If you withdraw K50, you receive exactly K50. We cover all transaction costs so you get your full earnings.

Important Tips to Get Paid Faster

1. Use one account only. Creating multiple accounts to do the same survey will get you banned and you will lose all earnings.

2. Answer surveys honestly. Companies check for fake answers. If you rush or lie, your survey will be rejected and you won’t be paid for it.

3. Keep your number active. If your MTN or Airtel line is barred or inactive, the payment will fail and be returned to us.

When Are New Surveys Available?

New surveys are added every week, usually on Mondays and Thursdays. High-paying surveys between K20-K25 go fast. We recommend logging in at least 3 times per week to check. Turning on browser notifications means you’ll be alerted as soon as a new survey matching your profile is available.

TaskEarn was built for Zambians, by Zambians. We know how important fast, reliable mobile money payments are. If you have any issues withdrawing, contact us through the form on the homepage and we’ll help within 24 hours.
