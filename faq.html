5. Why was I rejected from a survey?

This is normal. Always answer screening questions honestly. If you lie to qualify, the survey company will detect it and you won’t get paid.

No. One person = one account. Creating multiple accounts is fraud. Our system detects duplicate accounts using your device and IP address. If you are caught, all your accounts will be banned and you will lose all earnings. It’s not worth the risk.
